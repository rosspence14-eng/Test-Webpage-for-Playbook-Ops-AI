Who It's For

PlaybookOps is purpose-built for businesses that want operations to run without relying on memory, heroics, or constant explanation.

PlaybookOps helps teams capture how work actually happens, turn it into execution-ready SOPs, and keep work running reliably.

This Is For You If...

  • You're running a small to medium-sized business (5–250 people)
  • Critical work still lives in people's heads
  • Training depends on shadowing or "just ask them"
  • You're scaling, delegating, or preparing for leadership transitions
  • You want systems to run the business — not just document it

This Is Probably Not For You If...

  • You treat documentation as a writing project — not a living system
  • Your process stays stable for 12+ months and doesn't need ongoing updates
  • Binders, PDFs, and "set-it-and-forget-it" docs meet your needs
  • You only need compliance checkboxes and not operational execution
  • You want "AI magic" without defining owners, steps, or standards

Why This Matters

PlaybookOps isn't about documenting work — it's about making work repeatable without relying on memory. With structured SOPs, training, and workflows, teams stop depending on a few people and start depending on systems.
