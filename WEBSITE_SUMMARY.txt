WEBSITE OVERVIEW
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
PlayBookOps is a business operations platform designed to help companies 
transform their undocumented, knowledge-based processes into structured, 
repeatable systems. The website emphasizes turning tribal knowledge into 
execution-ready Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) through conversational 
AI capture.

5. WHO-ITS-FOR.HTML
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Purpose: Define target audience and use cases

Tagline: "Purpose-built for businesses that want operations to run without 
relying on memory, heroics, or constant explanation"

This Is For You If:
  • You're running a small to medium-sized business (5–250 people)
  • Critical work still lives in people's heads
  • Training depends on shadowing or "just ask them"
  • You're scaling, delegating, or preparing for leadership transitions
  • You want systems to run the business — not just document it

This Is Probably Not For You If:
  • You treat documentation as a writing project — not a living system
  • Your process stays stable for 12+ months without updates
  • Binders, PDFs, and "set-it-and-forget-it" docs meet your needs
  • You only need compliance checkboxes, not operational execution
  • You want "AI magic" without defining owners, steps, or standards

Why This Matters:
PlaybookOps makes work repeatable without relying on memory. Teams stop 
depending on a few people and start depending on systems.

                                   SUMMARY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The PlayBookOps website is a comprehensive marketing site for a SaaS platform 
that converts conversational knowledge capture into structured operational 
systems. The site follows a logical progression from problem identification 
(index), through solution explanation (about, how-it-works), audience 
qualification (who-its-for), proof points (proof), and conversion paths 
(pricing, build-sop).

The messaging consistently emphasizes the transition from memory-dependent, 
founder-centric operations to system-driven, repeatable execution. Every page 
reinforces the core value proposition: turn conversations into execution-ready 
SOPs without manual documentation work.

The site targets SMBs (5-250 employees) experiencing growing pains related to 
knowledge transfer, training efficiency, and operational consistency. Primary 
CTAs drive users to either book a demo or start creating their first SOP in 
the web application.

Technical implementation uses modular components (header/footer), dynamic 
content loading for legal pages, and custom CSS/JavaScript for visual effects. 
The overall design appears clean and professional, focusing attention on clear 
messaging and conversion paths.
