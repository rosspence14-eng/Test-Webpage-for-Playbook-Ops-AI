What PlayBookOps Is

PlayBookOps is the fastest way to turn “how we actually do things” into clean, usable SOPs—just by talking.

Explain a process out loud, and the system instantly converts it into steps, owners, tools, and training materials your team can follow.

It’s how businesses stop running on memory and start running on repeatable systems.

What It Does

  • Captures real process knowledge from conversation.
  • Structures work into steps, owners, tools, and standards.
  • Converts SOPs into training and repeatable execution.
  • Keeps workflows current as your business changes.
